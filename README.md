# Trading
- Trading strategy integrates both qualitative and quantitative approaches.
- On the qualitative side, I apply a top-down macro framework combined with event-driven analysis to evaluate economic growth conditions, identify emerging industry trends, and assess corporate strategies, products, and competitive positioning.
- On the quantitative side, I employ regression-based modeling alongside CAPM-informed portfolio construction to pursue superior risk-adjusted returns, particularly in volatile market environments. Alpha signals are generated through statistical regression and macro-driven analysis, while portfolio allocation is dynamically optimized to balance systematic beta exposure with alpha opportunities. Advanced risk management techniques — including Value-at-Risk (VaR), Conditional VaR (CVaR), and scenario-based stress testing — are incorporated to monitor tail risk and enhance overall portfolio stability.
- In addition, discretionary trading overlays are applied to capitalize on macro regime shifts and event-driven catalysts, enabling the strategy to remain adaptive across different market cycles.
